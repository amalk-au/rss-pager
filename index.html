Your receiver, explained.

              

                In Settings, connect a public RSS or Atom feed, or try BBC News,
                Hacker News, or The Register.
              

              

                ← / → select messages. ↑ / ↓ turn pages. The round read button
                advances a page, then a message. Scroll over the reading screen
                to do both.
              

              

                Open Inbox to filter by channel, select a message, or open its
                original article. Settings contains feed removal, sound,
                automatic refresh, and relay configuration.
              

              

                Feeds, up to 500 messages, and preferences stay in this browser.
                Existing saved feeds are migrated automatically on the same
                website origin. Refresh imports up to 50 entries per feed;
                article text is limited to 14,000 characters.
              

              

                GitHub Pages serves static files. Publishers that block browser
                access need a working CORS relay. Saved messages remain readable
                when offline.
